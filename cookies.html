Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, how we and our advertising partners use them, and the choices available to you.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile) when you visit a website. They help the website remember your actions and preferences and allow advertising partners to deliver relevant ads.

2. How We Use Cookies

Memo Notepad uses the following types of cookies:

  • Essential / Functional Cookies: Necessary for the website to function properly. These may remember preferences such as theme settings so the site works the way you expect.
  • Advertising Cookies: Used by Google AdSense and its partners to serve advertisements on our pages. These cookies help show relevant ads based on your visits to this site and other sites across the internet.

3. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising Cookies

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user’s prior visits to this website and/or other websites on the Internet.

Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google’s Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can visit www.aboutads.info to opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies for personalized advertising.

For more information about how Google uses data, please see How Google uses data when you use our partners’ sites or apps.

4. Your Notes Remain Private

Cookies and advertising technologies are never used to access, read, or collect the content of your memos or diary entries. Those remain stored only in your browser’s local storage on your device.

5. Your Cookie Choices

You have control over cookies. Most browsers allow you to:

  • View the cookies stored on your device
  • Block all or specific types of cookies
  • Delete existing cookies
  • Receive a notification when a new cookie is set

Blocking essential cookies may affect certain features. The core notepad and diary tools will still function because they rely primarily on local storage rather than cookies.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, legal requirements, or our practices. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
